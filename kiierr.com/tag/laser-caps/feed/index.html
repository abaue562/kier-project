What is a Laser Hair Growth Cap?

Have you begun experiencing hair loss and started looking around for solutions? You may have come across something known as a laser cap. How Does it Work?

Laser-based devices use LLLT technology. This means that lower-strength lasers emit photons that penetrate the scalp to increase adenosine triphosphate (ATP) production in follicle cells. In layman’s terms, the lasers stimulate the cells to increase blood flow and nutrient transport to grow more hair. The more you use it, the more blood flow will increase to your hair follicles and the more likely you are to see the results you are looking for. 

 

Can it Work for Me?

Hair loss is caused by a variety of factors. Some hair loss can be reversed by treatment methods while others may be permanent.  Hair loss that may be temporary can be caused by stress or trauma, medications or therapies, malnutrition from crash dieting or over exercising, childbirth or use of harsh hair care. 

LLLT can be beneficial for those who have mild to moderate male or female pattern hair loss, those who want to increase the thickness of their hair after hair loss, those who are trying to maintain hair-transplant results or anyone that is looking for a safe and practical way to regrow their hair. 

 

What are the Benefits of a Laser Cap over Other Methods?

The most popular options you have for hair loss treatment include medication, hair transplant surgery and laser therapy. 

The top medication for hair loss is Minoxidil (Rogaine). This medication can lead to unwanted side effects, including scalp irritation and unwanted hair growth on skin around the face and hands.  

Hair transplant surgery is the most common for permanent hair loss, however surgical procedures to treat hair loss and baldness are painful and not typically covered by insurance. 

Laser therapy, on the other hand, is safe, pain free and FDA approved. Laser caps give you the ability to treat your hair loss from anywhere, at any time. All you have to do is wear the cap and the LLLT does the work for you to revitalize damaged hair, promote healthier follicles and reverse hair loss.

It’s recommended that you use the laser cap for just 30 minutes, every other day. You don’t need to worry about wearing a laser cap every single day, all day long. The process is very simple and gives you the freedom to treat your hair loss on a regular basis from anywhere.

Results require consistency and patience. For those who are in the early to mid stages of hair loss, using a laser cap can yield results anywhere from six to twelve months. To maintain these results you should use the laser cap at least once or twice a week.

Understanding the Fitzpatrick Scale

Harvard dermatologist Thomas B. Fitzpatrick developed the Fitzpatrick scale in 1975. Today it is recognized as a standard tool in dermatology. So what exactly is it?

Essentially, it is a numerical scale that classifies skin type according to your pigmentation level and your skin’s reaction to ultraviolet sun rays. It was created to help people protect against skin cancer and other damage from the sun.

The more pigmentation your skin has (meaning the darker in color) the higher you are on the Fitzpatrick scale.

Types I, II and III all correspond to fairer skin tones, pale white to cream white. People who classify as type I, specifically, are characterized by ivory skin, light blue, gray, or green eyes, red or blond hair, and skin that always freckles, burns, or peels when exposed to the sun instead of tanning.

When it comes to type IV you can think of a Mediterranean complexion – olive or light brown skin that tans easily with dark brown eyes and hair. Finally, people who classify as types V and VI have dark brown skin that always tans, brownish black eyes, and dark brown to black hair.

What’s Behind the Myth that Laser Light Therapy Only Works on Light Skin?

So where does the misconception come from that Kiierr devices only work on light skin? Here’s where it gets interesting. Kiierr is FDA cleared for people with skin types I through IV on the Fitzpatrick scale. However, that’s only because there weren’t enough test subjects with skin types V and VI. This is actually the case for most clinical studies on the effectiveness of laser hair therapy.

As a result, some people incorrectly assume that LLLT only works for light to medium skin. In reality, it simply means there isn’t enough data yet on how well LLLT works for skin types V and VI to satisfy the FDA’s incredibly high standards. However, because of the way laser hair therapy works, it is scientifically more effective for people with darker skin tones. Let’s look at why that is.

Now, would you go outside wearing black clothes on a sunny, sweltering 100º F day? No, you wouldn’t because dark colors absorb more light, and thus more energy. When it comes to your comfort that’s a bad thing because more light = more energy = more heat.

When it comes to hair growth, however, this works in your favor! More light = more energy = more bio-stimulation at the mitochondrial level = more hair growth. And no need to worry about overheating if you choose a high-quality LLLT device.
