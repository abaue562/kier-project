Is Garlic Good for Hair Growth?

To answer this simply, yes, garlic works effectively and is good for hair growth. Whether you use it in your diet or use it topically, the properties present in garlic are tremendous boosters for the hair follicles on the scalp. Garlic’s nutritional content transfers to your hair and provides the nourishment it needs to prosper.

Raw garlic contains minerals, vitamins (B-6 and C), selenium, and manganese, all of which are essential promoters of hair growth. Each of these component benefits are discussed in greater detail below.

Garlic for Hair Growth Reviews and Published Studies

A study in 2016 revealed that garlic protects our keratinocytes from UV damage and also proved promising anti-aging material. We refer to keratinocytes as those skin cells that are responsible for keratin production. These cells are present on your skin’s outer layer, which includes your scalp and also in the hair follicles.

Another study in 2007 provided us with anecdotal proof that those who suffer from alopecia areata may improve their hair regrowth and minimize the bald patches if they apply garlic gel regularly. With such promising results and scientific evidence as a support, one cannot doubt that garlic is truly an effective hair growth remedy.

Garlic Benefits for Hair 

Here is a summary of the benefits of garlic for hair growth:

1.) Garlic Strengthens the Scalp

Bacteria and germs are among the top causes of damage to your scalp. , The presence of these two menaces, also inhibits your hair growth. Garlic contains antimicrobial properties that kill bacteria and germs and strengthens the quality of your scalp.

2.) Garlic Circulates Blood Flow

Proper blood circulation is an essential component of healthy hair growth. Garlic contains selenium content that boosts your blood circulation and maximizes nourishment. 

3.) Garlic Increases Collagen Production

There is a rich amount of Vitamin C content in raw garlic. This content not only encourages hair growth but also stimulates the production of collagen that further aids in hair growth.

4.) Garlic Eliminates Dandruff

Garlic is a soothing agent for an aggravated scalp and also works to eliminate dandruff. 

5.) Garlic Frees Follicles from Dirt and Grime

Garlic works to unclog your hair follicles, cleanses and strengthens them that slows down hair loss.

An Indian journal of Leprology, Venereology, and Dermatology published a study on the benefits of garlic for hair loss. According to the study, 5 percent of participants suffered from hair loss. After the application of garlic, the participants showed regrowth in the once bald areas, minimized bald spots, and an increased hair count. 

The study shows how the selenium and sulfur that garlic contains is effective in fortifying the structure of your hair shaft. This proves that garlic not only boosts hair growth but also can strengthen your existing hair.

How to Use Garlic for Hair Growth

1.) Garlic and Coconut Oil for Hair Growth

You will need some moderately warm coconut oil and minced garlic for this home remedy. Combine the two ingredients and apply the mixture to your scalp and hair. Allow it to remain for thirty minutes and then proceed to wash out.

You must note that you cannot use only garlic directly to your hair. Since the root vegetable packs a powerful punch, it could irritate your skin. You, therefore, need to add a carrier ingredient to minimize the likelihood of skin irritation. Apply this mixture thrice per week.

 

2.) Garlic with Rosemary For Hair Growth

To use garlic with rosemary, combine 5 tablespoons of garlic oil, 1 tablespoon of coconut oil, 1 tablespoon of castor oil and ½ tablespoon of rosemary oil. Combine all these ingredients in a jar and shake well.

Apply approximately 2 tablespoons of the blend to your scalp and massage thoroughly. Continue the massage for 15 minutes and then cover your entire hair with more oil. Allow it to sit for 30 minutes.

Proceed to wash out with a mild shampoo. You may use a conditioner, but it is optional since the oil mixture serves conditioning purposes too. It is best if you repeat this process thrice a week.

 

3.) Garlic with Honey

Combine honey and crushed garlic thoroughly in a bowl. Apply the mixture to your scalp and hair and allow it to remain for thirty minutes. Proceed to wash with a mild shampoo and running water. Apply this mixture twice a week for best results. 

 

4.) Garlic Oil for Hair

To prepare garlic oil at home, you will require 8 cloves of garlic, 1 medium onion (optional) and also ½ a cup of carrier oil (castor, coconut or olive oil etc.) To prepare the oil, you will have to crush the onion and garlic or put them into a blender for a fine pulp.

Once you have the pulp, heat the carrier oil in a pan and add the onion and garlic mixture to it. Once you see the pulp turning brown, you must switch off the heat. Allow the oil to cool down, then strain and discard the leftover pulp.

Apply 2 tablespoons of the mixture to your scalp and massage gently with it. Continue the massage for 15 minutes. Then apply more oil to the entire length of your hair. Allow it to sit  for 30 minutes then proceed to wash using a mild shampoo. Conditioner is optional.
