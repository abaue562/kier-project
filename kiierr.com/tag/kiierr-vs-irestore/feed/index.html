How Does Low-Level Light Therapy Work? 

LLLT therapy is designed to help hair follicles produce hair by triggering a number of useful processes. The laser light that is being used produces a particular wavelength of light. This wavelength is chosen because of its ability to be absorbed by the molecules inside hair follicles on the scalp. 

When the light is absorbed by the hair follicle molecules, the light then triggers the hair follicle to switch into the growth stage. It does this by signaling the stem cells to start regenerating the hair follicles, by boosting mitochondrial activity in the hair follicle cells, and by increasing blood flow, which helps deliver more oxygen and nutrients to the hair follicles. 

Researchers at Massachusetts General Hospital measured how closely blood vessel growth and blood flow are correlated when it comes to hair growth. They found that increased blood flow to the scalp resulted in blood vessel growth, which in turn resulted in hair growth. The reason that they are correlated is because the larger your blood vessels are in your scalp, the more oxygen and nutrients are able to be delivered. When these properties are being delivered, your hair follicles are healthier, and are able to work better. A low-level light therapy cap naturally stimulated the hair follicles, increasing blood flow, and encouraging new growth to begin. 

Scientific studies have been done to test the effectiveness of LLLT for hair regrowth. At least some studies have shown that it is capable of triggering hair regrowth for many people.

LEDs vs. Medical-Grade Lasers for LLLT 

Many laser cap and hair growth companies use both low-level lasers and light-emitting diodes (LEDs) in their hair growth caps. What many customers are not aware of, is that light-emitting diodes don’t produce the same stimulation of hair growth as medical-grade lasers. 

Laser caps are medical devices designed for men and women looking to improve the overall health of their hair. They are head-worn devices that look like a baseball cap that promote hair growth and stimulate your scalp by using laser diodes. These caps have hundreds of laser diodes that are designed to target every possible angle of your scalp, in order to ensure the best results. The idea behind these caps is based on research that has been conducted that has found that the exposure to low-level laser light stimulates cellular activity. By exposing the scalp and hair follicles to a concentrated laser light, the cap is able to target and promote hair growth and health. 

To create enhanced cell function, light energy needs to be below a specific energy threshold and within a specific wavelength. LEDs are typically less effective, because they are relatively low-powered and do not fit in between the specific threshold and wavelength that it takes to create this stimulation.

Because low-level laser light therapy involves the use of lasers, there have been extensive clinical trials to determine the safety and efficacy of this technology for hair loss treatments. Studies such as this one prove that LLLT is both effective and completely safe for hair loss treatment. During the clinical trials for this study, no laser hair cap side effects were observed in any of the hundreds of people who received the treatment.
