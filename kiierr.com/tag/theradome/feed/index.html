How to Use Theradome Laser Helmet

The Theradome laser helmet is cordless and lightweight. The helmet device has full scalp coverage and offers optimal scalp penetration to reach your hair follicles and encourage new growth. Their medical grade laser lights target the stem cells at the base of the hair follicles. This allows the formation of new photonic pathways to help restore your hair to a healthier state. Evidence suggests that LLLT acts on the mitochondria and may alter cell metabolism through photodissociation of inhibitory nitric oxide(NO) from cytochrome c oxidase(CCO), causing increased ATP production, modulation of reactive oxygen species, and induction of transcription factors. These transcription factors in return cause protein synthesis that triggers further effects down-stream, such as increased cell proliferation and migration, alteration in the levels of cytokines, growth factors and inflammatory mediators, and increased tissue oxygenation.

These helmet devices are FDA-cleared to provide safe and effective use for customers, and there’s no known side effects from using Theradome products.
