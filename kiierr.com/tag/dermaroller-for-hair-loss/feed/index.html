Derma Roller For Hair Loss Review

What is a derma roller for hair loss?

The derma roller, also known as micro needling, is a plastic tool that makes tiny pricks in your skin with its micro-needles. The titanium needles in this device cut into your dermal layer of the skin, piercing it just deep enough to boost the circulation and create new production of cells, encouraging hair growth in the process.

The sizes of pins vary between 0.2 to 2 millimeters. The derma roller for hair loss treatment is very simple, making it a very convenient hair loss solution to use while in the comfort of your own home.

There’s also the option of visiting a clinic where a medical professional can do it for you. Not only can a derma roller induce the collagen production to cure hair loss, but studies have shown that it can also cure imperfections on your skin as well.

Derma Roller for Hair Loss and the Science Behind It

When you roll the derma roller through your scalp, it creates a large number of tiny punctures all over the surface. Experts believe that the body reacts to these tiny wounds by flooding the subjected area with wound-healing and inflammatory molecules. This reaction stimulates the scalp to help boost new hair growth. Using the derma roller for hair loss can be an easy, safe, and effective method to help stimulate scalp hair growth for both men and women.

Microneedling is beneficial for stimulating the skin cell proliferation. Researchers have found that while microneedles have a positive effect on proliferating the skin cells and benefiting scars, wounds, and hyperpigmentation, the most effective use of microneedling is hair growth stimulation to prevent hair loss.

Derma rollers are not known to cause any damage to your scalp, the needles only penetrate deep enough into the scalp to trigger the proliferation of skin cells and initiate the healing process.

According to researchers, this process of healing includes three stages:

  • Inflammation
  • Proliferation
  • Maturation (remodeling)

How to Use a Derma Roller on Your Scalp

Pick the size of your needle

When researching micro-needling and hair loss, the web suggests a needle of 1.5 millimeters as the most common length for treating hair loss. This size coordinates with the depth of the penetration, as your hair follicles sit between 1 to 2 millimeters deep within your dermis. This is the exact depth of penetration that is required. Another option is using shorter needles, but they might not be as effective.

Establish a good wash routine and sterilize the derma roller

An exfoliating shampoo brush along with a thorough lathering and rinsing process will lift away all the dead skin and dirt present on the surface of one’s scalp. After washing, the derma roller including (the needles) need to be sanitized using rubbing alcohol.

Use a wide-tooth comb

Combing out all the tangles and knots is essential to allow the derma roller to cover all areas of the scalp when micro-needling.

Parting hair to allow unrestricted access

First, part your hair all the way to one side and roll. When this area is done, move to the next. Part your hair in sections to fully reach all areas of the scalp.

Use the derma roller horizontally

When micro-needling at home, work from side to side on each exposed portion of your scalp using a horizontal motion.

Maximize stimulation

Make sure you cover all areas of the scalp, go over each area several times before completing the treatment. Studies suggests ten rolls per one area. Using two different derma rollers to try and stimulate hair growth can also be effective. The difference in the rollers will be the size of the needles.

For example, as previously mentioned, the most common needle size to use in a derma roller for micro-needling purposes is 1.5 mm. However, evidence has shown that using rollers with different sized needles may help speed up the hair growth process.

Needles vary from 0.15 mm, 0.25 mm, 0.50 mm, 0.75 mm, 1.0 mm, and 1.5 mm. The derma rollers with smaller needles are known to help penetrate the scalp at the proper depth, whereas derma rollers with longer needles are designed to penetrate the scalp at a deeper level to boost cell production and prevent hair loss.

How Often Should You Derma Roll?

This varies depending on the size of needle. It is recommended when using a smaller needle less than 0.5 mm, to complete the treatment every other day, depending on your pain tolerance.

When using a longer needle size of approximately 1.5 mm, complete the treatment about once every week. Depending on one’s tolerance the amount of treatments may change. With a lower pain tolerance try applying less pressure when rolling the derma roller over the scalp. It’s important not to cause unbearable pain throughout this process.

Side Effects

The chances of side effects or damage using a derma roller to prevent hair loss are low. However, it is still possible for some people to experience the following side effects:

  • Pain
  • Bruising
  • Swelling
  • Redness

After a treatment, it’s common to experience inflammation and redness for a couple of days. Under certain conditions, scalps that are more sensitive may even experience scarring. The Institute of Dermatology states that most side effects subside within five days after treatment. One’s scalp may be extra sensitive to the sun after treatment. With a good sunscreen and taking the right precautions, there should be no problems.

Please consult a healthcare provider prior to using this treatment if you:

  • Suffer from a health condition that may obstruct healing: such as diabetes
  • Have a history of eczema or acne
  • Take blood thinners and other medications

Do Derma Rollers Work for Hair Loss?

The answer is YES! Derma rollers have been proven to be both a safe and effective solution for hair loss. This natural hair growth treatment has been known to produce hair growth results on the scalp when used consistently. Keep in mind, that your scalp might not react to the derma roller treatment the same way your neighbor’s does.

While this is a great solution to hair loss, make sure you find the right hair loss treatment that fits you, whether that be the derma roller, laser cap therapy, or vitamins and supplements.

How Long Does it Take to See Derma Roller Results? 

If you use the derma roller correctly and consistently, then it is possible to see derma roller results in as little as 8-10 weeks. This is considerably faster than certain other hair loss products, which can take 4-6 months, or even longer to show signs of progress. 

But just because results can start to be seen within 8-10 weeks doesn’t mean that you should stop using it then. For the best results, you should simply add the derma roller to your hair care routine and use it on an ongoing basis. Considering how easy it is to use, this should not be difficult for most people. 

Derma Roller Buying Guide 

When you are selecting a derma roller, you should take all of the following factors into consideration: 

  • Needle size – Derma rollers with longer needles don’t have to be used as frequently. However, they can cause more pain. Shorter needle sizes in the 0.225 mm – 1mm range cause less pain and many people believe they are more effective. But, derma rollers with shorter needles have to be used more frequently for the best results. 
  • Needle material – Titanium alloy is said to be one of the highest-quality materials for microneedles. So, you should prioritize derma rollers with this material.
  • Handle shape – Derma rollers with curved handles tend to be easier and more comfortable to use. Considering that you will have to use this product many times to get results, it is worthwhile to buy a derma roller with a comfortable curved handle shape.
