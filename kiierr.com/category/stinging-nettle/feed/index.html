The plant’s name is Latin in origin, with Uro meaning “I burn”. This is typically true since stinging nettle can potentially give you a burning rash if you fail to handle it properly. Nettle Leaf for Hair Possible Side Effects

If you use it in moderate amounts, you may find stinging nettle a relatively harmless herb. However, there can be certain side effects that you need to watch out for:

  • Rashes or hives
  • Urinary infections
  • Stomach issues
  • Diarrhea
  • Sweating

If you have any of these side effects, stop taking the supplement and talk to your doctor. You may need to take a smaller dosage if you begin experiencing any of these side effects.
