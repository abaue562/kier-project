There are two primary methods of hair transplant: Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT) and Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE). FUT involves removing a strip of scalp from the donor area and dissecting it into individual follicular units for transplantation. FUE, on the other hand, involves extracting individual follicular units directly from the donor area. The transplanted hair follicles take root in the recipient area and continue to grow naturally.

Hair transplant results are generally noticeable within several months, with full growth achieved within a year.

Stage of Hair Loss: LLLT is suitable for individuals in the early stages of hair loss or experiencing thinning. Hair transplant is typically recommended for individuals with stable hair loss patterns and sufficient donor hair.

 

Invasiveness: LLLT is a non-invasive treatment, while hair transplant is a surgical procedure that requires local anesthesia and a recovery period.

 

Maintenance: LLLT requires consistent and ongoing use to maintain results, while hair transplant results are generally permanent.
